---
title: Address types
---
Alamat Monero adalah yang Anda terbitkan/bagikan untuk menerima transaksi.\
Alamat dapat dibuat secara offline, secara gratis.

There are a few **types of public addresses** in Monero:

* [Standard address](./standard-address.md) - the wallet's primary address,
  often referred to as your "main" address.
* [Subaddress](./subaddress.md) - recommended address type.
* [Integrated address](./integrated-address.md) - some exchanges, merchants, and
  other businesses accepting Monero may opt to use these instead of
  subaddresses.
